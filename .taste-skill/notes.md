# Working notes (taste skill)

## Reading of the brief
Cold visitors from a short outreach email, on a phone, mostly businesses. The page must make one
thing clear in one screen: Levente comes once, scans the room with an iPhone's LiDAR, and you get a
measured floor plan and a 3D model in the browser where furniture and products sit at their real size.
One action: send an enquiry.

## Inventory (things only this client has)
1. The name: Plan 1:1. "1:1" means "exact / true to size" in all three languages
   (RO "unu la unu", HU "egy az egyhez", EN "one to one"), and 1:1 is the scale notation of a plan.
2. The real room: Levente's own bedroom, scanned on 29 October. 4.11 × 3.60 m overall, about 14.7 m²,
   ceiling 2.67 m. One door (69 cm), one window wall (2.14 m opening, blackout curtain), a 2.92 m
   wardrobe, a bed 1.96 × 2.34 m with a channelled headboard, two nightstands, a dresser, a vanity
   and chair.
3. The honest limit: RoomPlan only sees what the walk-through sees. The balcony behind the curtain
   came out as a plain window.
4. The product already modelled: Samsung 55″ QN85F, panel 122.8 × 70.6 × 4.6 cm, 76.1 cm tall on its
   stand, 17 kg, VESA 200 × 200, taken from the published drawing.
5. The viewer's own colour code on the floor plan: walls charcoal teal #293e42, windows blue #258eaf,
   doors orange #be753d, scanned pieces grey-blue/green/tan, added pieces yellow #f0d28c, floor #e7eae1.
6. The viewer's own artefacts: numbered legend "1. Bed · 196 × 234 cm", the tape-measure readout
   "1.234 m · horizontal … · vertical …", clearance labels in cm, named versions
   ("TV on dresser" is the viewer's own placeholder example), Undo.
7. The ritual: enquiry → written contract → scan visit (walk each room with the iPhone) →
   delivery and planning together.
8. The constraint: one person, no company, paid by bank transfer under a written contract,
   on-site visits by arrangement, Romania.
9. Three languages: Romanian, Hungarian, English (a Transylvanian reality).
10. The tools: iPhone Pro LiDAR, Apple RoomPlan, reference photos; the model opens in any browser,
    no install.
11. The trades' printed things: the tape measure, the measured survey (releveu) drawing,
    the cut list, the product datasheet with mm dimensions.

## Name
Plan 1:1. Rejected: "Interior Design Planner" (English only, and promises interior design, which
this is not); "Scanplan" (ScanPlan B.V., a Dutch point-cloud-to-floor-plan company, and Scan2Plan
in the US, work in exactly this field); "Releveu 3D" (implies a formal survey with DWG/PDF).

   render of the real bedroom, large; "Send an enquiry".
1. What you get: one visit gives a model, a plan with m², a tape measure and real-size planning
   → four real viewer images + the live thing (try the 55″ TV against the real bedroom wall).
2. Who it's for: each trade loses time to a different mistake; one scan removes it
   → five audiences, each with today / with a scan, each with its own anchor.
3. How it works: enquiry, contract, visit, delivery → four steps, the contract sentence verbatim.
4. Prices: introductory, below Matterport tours and releveu surveys → from-prices in lei,
