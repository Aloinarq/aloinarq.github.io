# How to get paid without a company: the route chosen

**This is not legal or tax advice. An accountant must check this note and the contract before you use them for the first time.** Figures are as published in the sources below (checked 19 November 2026); they change, so confirm them when you file.

## The route

One written contract per job, signed before any work: a **services contract** (*contract de prestări servicii*, the Civil Code's *contract de antrepriză*) **that also assigns the economic copyright** in the delivered models and plans (`contract-ro.md`; English and Hungarian reading copies beside it, the Romanian text prevails). Payment only by bank transfer to your own account.

By default use **Option A** in art. 3.1: one price, with the copyright assignment included. Option B (a separate copyright fee) is there only if your accountant agrees.

## Why this route

- **It is the route ANAF itself describes for occasional work.** ANAF Ploiești's guide on occasional services (18 Sept 2025) treats a *contract de antrepriză* done occasionally, without continuity, as *venituri din alte surse* (Fiscal Code art. 114(2)(g), Norms point 34(2)): 10% income tax, withheld by a payer that keeps accounts; you file the Declarația unică for it when the tax was not withheld, and when you owe health contribution (CASS).
- **The copyright clause is there for your clients, not for tax.** Agents need to publish your plan in a listing and designers need to show your model to their clients; the clause gives them that right in writing, with the elements Law 8/1996 requires (rights, means, territory, duration, fee).
- **Why not a copyright-only contract (cheaper on paper).** Copyright income is taxed at 10% of net income after a 40% flat deduction (6% of the gross), withheld by business payers (Keez, 2026). But the work must show the author's own creative choices, not routine or mechanical work. A measured as-built plan is mostly measurement, so presenting the whole fee as copyright invites requalification. That is why Option B splits the fee and needs your accountant's agreement.

## What you must do yourself

1. **Keep a file per job:** the signed contract, the bank statement showing the payment, and any withholding certificate from a business client.
2. **Declare the income in the Declarația unică (form D212)** for the income a private client paid you without withholding (and any income where tax was not fully withheld), by **25 May of the following year**: income earned in 2026 is declared and paid by **25 May 2027**. File through SPV on anaf.ro.
3. **Watch the CASS threshold.** Health contribution (10%) is due when your non-salary income from these sources reaches **6 gross minimum wages** in the year: 24,300 lei at the 4,050-lei minimum wage the 2026 figures use (ANAF Iași, Keez). Pension contribution (CAS) starts at 12 minimum wages for independent and copyright income. Confirm the minimum wage in force when you file.
4. **Register a PFA when the work becomes regular.** OUG 44/2008 requires economic activity carried out regularly to be registered (PFA, II or IF) before it starts; ANAF Iași notes that unauthorised economic activity is fined **2,000 to 15,000 lei** under Law 296/2023, with confiscation of the goods used. A public website and outreach to businesses are themselves signs of a regular activity, so treat the occasional route as a bridge for the first jobs only, and **ask your accountant at the start when to register**, not after the fact. With a PFA you would invoice through RO e-Factura and keep the PFA's books.
5. **VAT** is not a concern at this size (exemption threshold 395,000 lei a year).
6. **Free first room for businesses:** still sign the contract (for the data and copyright clauses), with a price of 0 lei.
