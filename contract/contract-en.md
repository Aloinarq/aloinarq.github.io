## Art. 2. Subject of the contract

2.1. The Provider undertakes to perform, independently, with his own equipment and at his own risk, the following services:

a) scanning, at ______________________________, the following rooms: ______________________________ (____ rooms in total, about ____ m²), with an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor and Apple's RoomPlan app, including reference photos of the rooms;

b) rebuilding each scanned room as a 3D model that opens in a web browser without installing an app, and producing the floor plan of the rooms, with walls, doors, windows and numbered furniture, the overall dimensions, the floor area in m² and a legend with the dimensions of the pieces;

c) [optional] assisted planning: placing in the model up to ____ pieces of furniture or products chosen by the Client, at the dimensions published by their makers;

d) one handover and planning session together [on site / online], of at most ____ minutes.

2.2. The deliverables ("**the Deliverables**") are: (i) a private link to the 3D model and floor plan of each room; (ii) [where applicable] the named arrangement versions.

2.3. Not included: PDF, CAD or DWG export, photoreal renders, scanning outdoor spaces or whole buildings, interior design. The Provider does not guarantee an accuracy expressed as a figure. The measurements are taken with the LiDAR sensor and are meant for planning; critical dimensions for execution (for example for made-to-measure furniture or worktops) are checked on site by the Client or the Client's contractor.

2.4. The scan captures only what is visible during the walk-through of the room. The Client opens or clears what must be visible (curtains, doors, wardrobes), and the Provider reports in writing any areas he could not capture.

2.5. The services are provided occasionally. There is no employment or subordination relationship between the parties.

## Art. 6. Assignment of the economic copyright

6.1. To the extent that the delivered 3D models, floor plans and arrangement versions are works protected by Law no. 8/1996 on copyright and related rights, the Provider, as author, assigns to the Client, on a [non-exclusive / exclusive] basis, the right to reproduce, distribute, communicate them to the public (including making them available to the public, for example in a property listing) and adapt them, by any means (digital, print, online), in [Romania / the whole world], for [____ years / the full term of protection], for the following purposes: ______________________________ [for example: designing and presenting the arrangement, offers to clients, sale or rental listings].

6.2. The remuneration for the assignment is the one in art. 3.1 (Option A: included in the price; Option B: the separately stated amount).

6.3. The Provider keeps the right to be recognised as the author and the right to use the software, methods and generic elements created independently of this contract. The Provider does not use the Deliverables or the Client's scan to present his own work (portfolio, website, social media) without the Client's prior written consent.

## Art. 7. Confidentiality and data protection

7.1. The scan, the reference photos, the models and the plans ("**the Scan Data**") belong to the Client. The Provider keeps them confidential, uses them only to perform this contract, does not publish them and does not pass them to anyone else, except the strictly necessary technical providers (hosting, email) acting on his behalf.

7.2. The Provider processes the Client's personal data, and any personal data that may appear in the Scan Data, as controller, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the privacy policy published at https://aloinarq.github.io/ro/confidentialitate/ (in English: https://aloinarq.github.io/en/privacy/).

7.3. The Provider deletes the Scan Data and deactivates the private link: (a) at the Client's written request (including by email), within ____ days, confirming the deletion in writing; or (b) when the period in art. 4.3 ends, unless the parties have agreed otherwise in writing. Only the documents the law requires the Provider to keep (the contract, proof of payment) are kept.

7.4. If people or personal documents appear by mistake in the reference photos, the Provider does not use them and deletes them before any other processing.

7.5. The duty of confidentiality continues after the contract ends.

## Art. 8. Delivery and acceptance

8.1. Delivery is made by sending the private link to the Client's email address and through the session in art. 2.1 (d).

8.2. Within ____ working days of delivery, the Client may report obvious errors in writing (for example a missing room or a wrong wall). The Provider corrects them free of charge within ____ working days if they can be corrected from the existing scan; if a new visit is needed for reasons on the Provider's side, it is free of charge.

8.3. If no errors are reported within the period in art. 8.2, the Deliverables are deemed accepted.
